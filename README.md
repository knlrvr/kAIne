# kAIne

**The AI assistant with zero artificial intelligence. Or any other kind.**

Everyone else is racing to build the smartest AI in the world. We don't do that here. 

kAIne is a chat app that looks, feels and loads exactly like a real AI assistant. It has the sidebar. It has the model picker. It has the little thinking dots. The only thing it left out is the thinking.

## Why kAIne?

- **Unshakeable confidence.** kAIne answers every question instantly and without hesitation. Whether the answer relates to your question is between you and kAIne.
- **Never hallucinates.** Hallucinating would require trying. Every response is hand-written nonsense, crafted by a human and delivered at random.
- **Total privacy.** kAIne doesn't read your messages. Not for training. Not for safety. Not even to answer them.
- **100% uptime on being wrong.** Competitors are wrong sometimes. We offer consistency.
- **No learning curve.** kAIne will not learn anything, and neither will you.
